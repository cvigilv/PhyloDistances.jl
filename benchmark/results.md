# Robinson-Foulds: PhyloDistances.jl vs TreeDist

Generated by `julia --project=benchmark benchmark/run.jl`.

Both implementations read the same Newick files from `benchmark/trees/`, 
and neither timing includes parsing — only the distance computation.

- Julia: minimum of a `BenchmarkTools` trial.
- R: median of repeated calls, looped until the clock resolution stops mattering.

**Memory is measured differently on each side and the two columns are not 
comparable.** Julia reports bytes allocated by the call; R reports the peak 
its garbage collector saw, which includes everything already resident.

## Environment

- Julia 1.12.6 on arm64-apple-darwin24.0.0
- TreeDist 2.14.1 | ape 5.8.1 | R 4.4.2
